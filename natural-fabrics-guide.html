A Guide to Natural Fabrics

Natural fibres breathe, soften with wear and can be repaired again and again. Here is how the main ones feel, wear and like to be cared for.

1Linen

Made from flax, linen is cool, strong and gets softer with every wash. It creases easily, which is part of its charm. Wash cool, dry flat or on a line, and iron while still slightly damp if you prefer a crisp look.

2Cotton

Soft, breathable and easy to wash, cotton is the backbone of most wardrobes. Look for long-staple cotton for shirts and tees that pill less and hold their shape. Wash at 30 to 40°C and avoid high tumble heat to limit shrinking.

3Wool

Wool keeps you warm even when damp and resists odours, so it needs less washing. Air it between wears, hand wash or use a wool cycle with a gentle detergent, and dry flat. Merino is fine and soft enough to wear next to the skin.

4Silk

Silk is light, smooth and warm for its weight. It is stronger than it looks but dislikes heat, sunlight and harsh detergents. Hand wash cool with a mild detergent, roll in a towel to remove water and press on a low setting.

5Hemp and newer fibres

Hemp is hard-wearing and feels like a sturdier linen. Lyocell, made from wood pulp, drapes beautifully and is often blended with cotton or linen.

  • Check the care label: blends follow the most delicate fibre.
  • Wash less, air more.
  • Mend small holes early.
