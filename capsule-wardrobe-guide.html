Building a Capsule Wardrobe

A capsule wardrobe is a small collection of clothes that all work together. It makes getting dressed easier and helps each piece earn its place.

1Start by editing

Take everything out and sort it into three piles: wear often, wear sometimes and never wear. Be honest about fit and comfort. Keep the first pile, think hard about the second, and pass on the third through friends, swaps or charity.

2Choose a quiet palette

Pick two or three base colours, such as navy, grey, cream or black, then one or two accents you love. When everything shares a palette, almost any top works with any bottom, and the number of outfits multiplies.

3Count your core pieces

A seasonal capsule often holds 25 to 40 pieces including shoes. A useful starting shape:

  • Five to eight tops
  • Three to five bottoms
  • Two or three layers, such as a knit and a jacket
  • Two or three pairs of shoes

4Fill the gaps slowly

Live with the edited wardrobe for a few weeks before buying anything. Note what you miss, not what you see in shops. When you do buy, look for natural fabrics, good seams and a fit that needs no excuses.

5Rotate with the seasons

Store out-of-season clothes clean and folded, with cedar or lavender to deter moths. When the season turns, swap them in and edit again. Each rotation is a chance to notice what you truly wear.
